chapter two · the letter

Happy birthday, my love. Somewhere between the ordinary Tuesdays and the trips we still talk about, you became the person I measure every good thing against. Today isn't really about the cake, though we'll have that too — it's about pausing the whole world for a minute to say that you, exactly as you are, are the best part of my life.

You have this way of making rooms feel warmer just by walking into them. I don't think you know how often I'm quietly grateful for that — for the way you laugh with your whole self, the way you show up for the people you love without needing to be asked twice, the way you make even the smallest plans feel like adventures. I fell for all of it, slowly and then all at once, and I haven't stopped since.

So here's to another year of choosing each other on purpose. Here's to more late nights that turn into early mornings, more silly arguments about nothing that end in laughing, more of your hand finding mine without either of us thinking about it. Wherever this year takes us, I already know the best part of it will be you.
